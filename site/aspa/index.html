ASPA lets an AS publish, in the RPKI, which ASes are its upstream providers, so routers can spot paths that could not have happened (route leaks and some hijacks). Until this site publishes its own verdicts, these numbers come live from the free BGPKIT API.

Top invalid customer / provider pairs

The customer AS's ASPA does not list the provider it sent the route to, and that hop breaks ASPA for the path. This can be a route leak, or an ASPA that is missing a provider. Counted over every path in this portal's own RIS and RouteViews feeds, refreshed hourly.
